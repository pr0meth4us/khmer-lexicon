What this is

A place to look up the official Khmer word for a term — the word a ministry or the National Council of Khmer Language has published for things like blockchain, governance or contract.

It holds {{ '{:,}'.format(about.entries) }} words from {{ sources|length }} government documents. Every word shows which document it came from and the year, so you can always go back to the original.

Look up a word · ស្វែងរក

  • Type in Khmer, English or French.
  • You don't have to spell it perfectly. If nothing matches, you'll see the closest words instead.
  • No Khmer keyboard? Type how the word sounds in Latin letters — tinnany finds ទិន្នន័យ.
  • ចម្លង (copy) puts the Khmer word on your clipboard, ready to paste.

Browse · រកមើលតាមប្រធានបទ

See every word from one document, or every word starting with a given letter.

Check a letter · ពិនិត្យលិខិត

Paste a draft and press ពិនិត្យលិខិត. It will:

  • highlight the official terms you used;
  • suggest the official Khmer word where you wrote an English one;
  • point out hidden characters that look like Khmer but aren't — they are invisible on screen, but they stop a word being found or sorted properly.

It does not check general spelling or grammar. It only knows the official terms, so ordinary words are not checked at all.

Can I trust it?

Use it as a helper, not as the final word. The documents were scanned paper, and a computer read them, which introduces mistakes.

If a word matters — in an official letter, a law, a publication — check it in the original document. Each entry tells you which one.

Found a mistake?

Press រាយការណ៍កំហុស (report) under the word, or tell us here. No account needed, and you can write in Khmer. Every report makes the dictionary better.
